Full freeform copper-wire satellite stand with structural GND frame, signal buses, and 18650 battery base.
1. Copper Base & 18650 Cradle 1.2mm Wire
Bend 1.2mm (16 AWG) bare copper rod into the 84×58mm base rectangle with twin Ø19mm rear hoops to cradle the 18650 cell.
2. Spine Risers & YD-ESP32-23 GND Frame
Solder two vertical copper pillars from the base up into ESP32-S3 corner GND pins (L22, R1, R21, R22). Leave 18mm bottom clearance for USB-C.
3. Left Wing: I²S Microphone Pod GPIO 4/5/6
Extend a top copper GND arm left at Y=69mm to bridge Mic GND & L/R (Left channel). Solder VDD→3V3, SCK→4, WS→5, SD→6.
4. Right Wing: MAX98357A Amp GPIO 15/16/17
Mount purple amp via corner holes & GND pin on right wing. Route parallel copper bridge behind ESP32-S3 from GPIO 15/16/17 & 5Vin.
5. Central Speaker Cradle SPK+ / SPK−
Seat the 30×40×14mm cavity speaker inside the rear copper basket. Connect Red→SPK+ and Black→SPK− (never connect SPK− to GND!).
6. 18650 Battery & 5V Boost Rail Power Bus
Place 18650 cell in rear rings. Connect B+/B− through a 5V Boost/Charger module + switch to feed the 5Vin rail and copper GND frame.

Real-World Soldering Notes:
1. Speaker− is differential: Solder Black speaker wire ONLY to MAX98357A SPK−, never to the copper GND frame.
2. 18650 Voltage (3.7V–4.2V): Route the 18650 through a 5V boost/charger module to 5Vin, or use USB-C power while testing. Never connect raw 4.2V to 3V3.
3. Clearance: Step the rear copper signal bus 6mm behind the ESP32-S3 PCB so bare copper wires never touch each other or the GND frame.
